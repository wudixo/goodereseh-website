Cookie Policy

This policy explains how goodereseh.com may use cookies and similar technologies.

Current rule: strictly necessary cookies may operate without consent where legally permitted. Non-essential cookies should not be set until the visitor has given valid consent.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on or accessed from your device when you use a website. Similar technologies can perform related functions.

2. Strictly necessary technologies

Some technologies may be required for core website functions such as security, maintaining a checkout session, remembering an essential user action or protecting forms. Where a technology is strictly necessary to provide a service you requested, consent may not be required.

3. Analytics, advertising and other optional technologies

If Good Ereseh uses analytics, advertising, personalisation or other non-essential cookies or similar technologies, they should be disabled until you have made an appropriate consent choice.

4. Third-party services

The website uses external services for functions such as payments, AI, hosting, databases and email. Some providers may set or use cookies or similar technologies when their service is loaded. Their own policies may apply to those technologies.

5. Cookie consent

Where consent is required, it should be given through a clear positive choice. Simply continuing to browse the website should not be treated as consent to non-essential cookies.

You should be able to reject or disable non-essential cookies as easily as you can accept them.

6. Current cookie list

The exact cookie list depends on the technologies currently active on the site. Good Ereseh will maintain this policy so that it reflects the actual cookies and similar technologies in use. Before enabling analytics or advertising tools, the cookie banner and this page should be updated to identify the provider, purpose and duration of each non-essential cookie.

7. Browser controls

You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some strictly necessary technologies may prevent parts of the site from working correctly.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when website technologies change.

9. Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent to art@goodereseh.com.
