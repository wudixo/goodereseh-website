How it works

The process.

01
Consultation

We discuss your idea, preferred artwork size, reference images and the feeling you want the artwork to communicate. No commitment required at this stage.

02
Creation

The artwork is carefully developed through layers of charcoal and graphite to build realism, depth and detail. You receive progress updates throughout.

03
Completion

Your finished original artwork is reviewed, signed and prepared for secure delivery in a black presentation box with Certificate of Authenticity and wax seal.
