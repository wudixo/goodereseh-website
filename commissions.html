A Portrait With Meaning

Every commission begins with a personal story. Whether it is a family portrait, a memorial piece, a gift for someone special or a personal collector artwork, each piece is carefully created to preserve emotion and character.

Using traditional charcoal and graphite techniques, each artwork is drawn entirely by hand with attention to detail, texture and expression.
