Reflections

Original Hyperrealistic Eye Drawing

A large-scale graphite and charcoal artwork capturing the complexity, emotion and realism found within the human eye.

About The Artwork

Reflections explores the relationship between vision, emotion and human experience through a single powerful focal point. The eye becomes a symbol of identity, perception and connection.

Created entirely by hand using graphite and charcoal pencil, every eyelash, reflection and texture has been carefully observed and recreated. The artwork combines photographic realism with the character of traditional fine art drawing.

Completed over 35 hours, this statement piece was created for collectors who appreciate detailed handmade artwork and traditional drawing techniques.
