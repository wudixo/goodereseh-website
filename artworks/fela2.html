Portrait · Charcoal Study

The
Stance

A powerful half-length study of Fela Anikulapo Kuti, rendered in bold charcoal with striking contrast and texture.

This original charcoal portrait captures Fela Anikulapo Kuti in a commanding, arms-folded stance, draped in a richly textured, patterned garment. The interplay of deep shadow and raw, energetic mark-making gives this piece a sculptural, almost dimensional quality. Every fold of fabric and line of expression was built up entirely by hand in charcoal.

Unlike a straightforward likeness, this portrait leans into atmosphere and presence — capturing not just Fela’s features but his unmistakable poise and confidence. The result is a moody, richly detailed tribute to a cultural icon.

The original is sold. Limited edition fine art prints were available on archival cotton paper. To be notified of future works, get in touch.
