Fela Kuti

Original Hand-Drawn Portrait

A hyperrealistic tribute to the father of Afrobeat, created entirely by hand using graphite and charcoal pencil.

About The Artwork

This portrait celebrates Fela Anikulapo Kuti, the Nigerian musician, activist and creator of Afrobeat. The artwork captures his character, presence and individuality through carefully layered graphite and charcoal techniques.

Every detail, from skin texture to facial expression, was studied and recreated by hand. The result is a traditional fine art portrait that preserves the spirit and cultural influence of one of Africa's most recognised musical figures.

Completed over 38 hours, this original artwork represents a personal interpretation of Fela's identity and legacy. This one-of-a-kind original artwork is available for collectors.
