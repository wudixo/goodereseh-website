Fela Kuti: Original Hand-Drawn Portrait

A striking hyperrealistic tribute to the father of Afrobeat, hand-rendered in graphite and charcoal.

This original portrait honors Fela Anikulapo Kuti, the Nigerian musical icon and creator of Afrobeat, captured mid-expression in a moment of pure charisma. Drawn entirely by hand using graphite and charcoal pencil, this piece showcases exceptional realism; from the texture of the skin to the light reflecting in his eyes.

Every mark on the page was placed with intention over 38 hours of continuous work, resulting in a one-of-a-kind collector's piece that brings warmth, character, and cultural history into any space. This is not a print or reproduction; it is a unique, original drawing that will never be recreated again.
