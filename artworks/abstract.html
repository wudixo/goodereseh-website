Fragments of Thought: Abstract Charcoal Figure Study

An expressive charcoal study exploring emotion, movement and human presence through spontaneous mark-making.

Fragments of Thought explores the relationship between simplicity and expression. Through intersecting charcoal strokes, the figure emerges from the page as a study of emotion, movement and instinct.

Rather than focusing on traditional realism, this artwork embraces the raw character of charcoal. Every mark contributes to the feeling of energy, creating a contemporary piece designed for collectors who appreciate expressive handmade artwork.
