The artist

Made by
hand.
Felt by
all.

Good Ereseh is a hyperrealistic charcoal and graphite portrait artist based in London. Every mark is made by hand on 100% cotton paper — no digital processes, no shortcuts.

The work spans two distinct bodies: hyperrealistic portrait studies that capture people with photographic precision, and the Beyond the Seen series — conceptual line works that ask the viewer to finish the drawing with their own imagination.
